# Ideas, series and the calendar (reach method)

## Reference bank (research)
- Goal: ≥ 60 strong references over time, of different kinds (educational, story, demo, transformation, comparison, mistakes, behind the
  scenes, opinion, customer story, entertaining). Keep it in a sheet: link · views · likes · comments · shares · saves · length ·
  FIRST 3 SECONDS · why I stopped (curiosity / relatable / funny / useful / controversial / strong visual / bold claim / story / fast
  pacing / surprise) · format · idea I can use · STRONG = share rate or save rate ≥ 1% of views.
- Search like a normal person (2-5 words), search the viewer's problems / desires / mistakes / fears, go one level broader and into
  adjacent niches; judge by evidence (numbers vs the account's normal), not by taste. Never invent creator names.
- Aly's adjacent niches worth studying (to verify by searching): science/explainer creators, history-map channels, film-analysis
  creators, tech reviewers / «I tested», design and motion creators, Arabic educational shorts, career/freelance creators.
- When studying a video, really study it (transcript + frames + packaging — `studying-videos.md` in aly-content-assistant).
- Per creator: top 5 videos + thinking layer (why it performed) → synthesis: patterns, hook style, best angle, copy / avoid, the GAP,
  his unique angle, first 3 videos, hypotheses.

### Search-map prompt (adapted for Aly, any chat model)
```
Act as my content research assistant. Goal: find HIGH-PERFORMING TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts videos I can study.
Don't make searches too specific; performance first, relevance second.
What I make: [topic of this batch, e.g. Egyptian history with maps / AI video tools / video editing]
Who watches: Egyptian and Arab viewers 16-35 interested in [..]
Problem I solve / value: [..]   Country / language: Egypt / Egyptian Arabic (+ English searches)
Give: A) 5 broad topic searches B) 5 viewer-problem searches C) 5 viewer-desire searches D) 3 adjacent niches × 3 searches + why
E) 10 format searches (before/after, mistakes, things nobody tells you, how it's made, comparison, myth vs fact, ranking, experiment,
storytime, reaction) F) 5 creator TYPES (no invented names). Then the TOP 15 searches ranked by: many videos, high performers,
usefulness, ease. Searches 2-5 simple words. No ideas, no scripts — only searches.
```

## From references to ideas (the content factory)
1. Reference → «what happens in one sentence» → split FORMAT / TOPIC.
2. Remove the topic, keep the format with a blank (×5 references).
3. Drop Aly's topic into each blank.
4. Each good format → 5 episodes (same format, new angle).
5. SERIES TEST: can it make 5-10 episodes? Yes = series (name it). No = one-off (allowed for A content, but series win).
6. Bank: 3 series × 5 = 15+ ideas. Then filter through his content-skill questions (what does Aly add that doesn't exist in Arabic? does
   it use his unfair advantages? would he be excited? can he make it in a week?). Repeat the FORMAT, never the content.

## The calendar (ESMA)
- Aly now: 55% A / 35% B / 10% C (awareness bottleneck). Re-decide every quarter with the rules in `method.md` §2.
- Spread letters through the week (A B A C A B A …); no C blocks; long Friday video = B, its shorts carry the A slots during the week.
- Example week at 5 posts: Fri long (B) · Sat short A · Mon short B · Wed short A · Thu short A (+ a C every other week).
- Trend vs evergreen ≈ 20/80.
