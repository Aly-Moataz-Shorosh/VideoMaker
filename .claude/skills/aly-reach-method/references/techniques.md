# Scripting techniques library (Homus, rewritten for Aly)

Tools, not rules. Pick the few the idea needs. The only job at every second of a video: give the viewer ONE reason to keep watching.
Each: what it is · Aly-world example (Egyptian colloquial) · best place · the trap.

## 01 Attention — hook techniques (first 1-3 s, scene changes)

1. **Pattern interruption** — break what the scroller expects, visually or verbally, in the first 1-3 s.
   Ex: the first frame = a perfect «AI» Cairo street that suddenly glitches, «الفيديو ده مفيهوش ولا لقطة اتصورت».
   Place: first seconds, scene transitions, breaking a monotone middle. Trap: an interrupt with no link to the topic = cheap; it must
   connect immediately.
2. **Open loop** — start an idea and don't finish it; the viewer stays to close it.
   Ex: «في حاجة واحدة في الفيديو ده كلفتني أكتر من كل الأدوات مع بعض… هقولك هي ايه في الآخر».
   Place: hook, story transitions, before a key insight. Trap: a loop never closed = broken trust. Every loop pays off before the end
   (mark `[لوب مفتوح]` / `[لوب مقفول]`).
3. **Specificity** — exact details instead of vague claims; feels real.
   Ex: «5 أيام، 11 نسخة، 39 credit» not «اتعب فيه شوية». Place: hooks, proof, results. Trap: invented precision kills trust —
   real numbers or none (his sourcing rules apply).
4. **Framing the stakes** — what the viewer LOSES if they skip this.
   Ex: «لو بتدفع اشتراك ChatGPT وبتعمل كده، انت بترمي نص الفلوس». Place: hooks, before key insights.
5. **Reframing** — a familiar idea from an unexpected angle.
   Ex: «المونتاج مش قص ولزق، المونتاج انك تقرر المشاهد مش هيشوف ايه». Place: hooks, explainers, opinions. Trap: a weak reframe sounds
   like a generic quote — skip it if it isn't genuinely surprising.
Reminder: the hook's job is not to be clever; it is to make leaving feel like a loss.

## 02 Retention — body techniques

1. **Micro-hooking** — a new small reason to stay every 10-15 s (shorts) / re-hook every 60-90 s plus small ones (long videos).
   Ex: «انت فاكر ان دي المشكلة؟ لأ. المشكلة الحقيقية…». Trap: putting all the effort in the hook.
2. **Progressive reveal** — give it piece by piece; make them earn each piece.
   Ex: «في 3 طرق: الأولى اللي الكل بيعملها، التانية اللي بتشتغل فعلا، والتالتة اللي بتغير كل حاجة».
3. **Mini reset** — briefly re-anchor attention without leaving the topic. Ex: «بس هنا بقى الناس بتقع». Trap: >2-3 per video = formula.
4. **Escalation** — stakes rise as the video goes; it should feel like it's building to something.
   Ex: «مش موضوع مجهود. ولا موضوع وقت. الموضوع اتجاه، ودي الحتة اللي محدش بيتكلم فيها».
5. **Embedded curiosity** — a second question inside the main idea.
   Ex: «الطريقة دي بتشتغل. بس السبب اللي بيخليها تشتغل أهم من الطريقة نفسها».
6. **Rhythm variation** — vary sentence length; short = speed, long = build. Ex: «ناس كتير بتستنى. بتتردد. بتراجع نفسها. ولما تجهز،
   تكون الفرصة راحت.» Trap: same sentence length everywhere = monotone content. (Matters doubly for the AI twin voice — it flattens rhythm.)
7. **Contrast** — two approaches / behaviours / outcomes side by side. Ex: «المبتدئ بيدور على أداة أحسن. المحترف بيدور على workflow».
   Place: concepts, mistakes, comparisons (our split-screen / before-after boards).

## 03 Clarity & trust

1. **Credibility insertion** — proof at the exact moment the viewer may doubt, not all at the start.
   Ex (right after a bold claim): «جربت ده على 34 شورت من الكورس، والنتيجة كانت نفسها كل مرة». Trap: front-loading credentials.
2. **Objection handling** — name and kill the reason to dismiss you. Ex: «مش محتاج كارت شاشة غالي. ولا خبرة. محتاج نقطة بداية،
   ودي هي». Place: beginners, tutorials, offers/C content.
3. **Fast proof** — result before process (our «hook = the before→after demo» rule, style 11). Trap: implied proof without substance.
4. **Compression** — cut every word with no weight. «عايز آخد دقيقة أشرح حاجة مهمة» → «دي مهمة». Trap: compress until meaning is lost.
Reminder: trust is built in the moment of doubt, not in the intro.

## 04 Flow & structure

1. **Delayed labeling** — show/experience the idea, THEN name it. Matches Aly's rule «concepts before names» (style 27 v11): show what a
   transcript does with a tiny visual, then say «ده اسمه Transcription».
2. **Clean exit** — end right after the last value. No summary, no «وده كان كل حاجة النهارده», no long goodbye. In shorts: last value →
   CTA line → end (loop if possible). In his long videos keep the signature close SHORT.
3. **Signposting** — tell them where they are and what's next. Ex: «دي المشكلة. طب ليه الحل المعروف مبيشتغلش، وايه اللي بيشتغل».
   Our visual form of this: breadcrumb «family › sub» + TreeMap separators (style 27).

## Quick script template (one card per video)
- Core idea: one sentence — who · result · method.
- Hook: 2-3 versions → pick one (pause test).
- Key points: in delivery order.
- Micro-hooks: where you reset / layer curiosity (every 10-15 s in shorts).
- Proof / credibility: where it goes (at the doubt moments).
- CTA: one line, only after the final point (if needed).
