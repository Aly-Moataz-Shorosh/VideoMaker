# Writing a script with the reach method (short or long)

Works WITH Aly's content skill, never against it: his voice (`voice-and-style.md`), one promise per video, packaging first, his shows'
fixed segments, his sourcing rules (`research-and-sourcing.md`) — all in
`E:/root/output/_claude/Aly Shorosh - Content Creator/.claude/skills/aly-content-assistant/references/`. Read voice + feedback-log there
when writing in his voice. This file adds the reach layer on top.

## Step 0 — decide the job of the video (before any line)
Write this card at the top of every script and show it to Aly:
```
النوع: A (وصول) / B (ثقة) / C (فعل)        ← who is it for: stranger / knows me / ready to act
الفورمات: ...   (proven shape: 3 أخطاء، X ضد Y، قبل/بعد، هتصدقني لو، ترتيب، اللي محدش بيقولهولك، اتعمل ازاي ...)
السلسلة: ...    (which series it belongs to — can this format make 5-10 episodes?)
الفكرة في جملة: مين + النتيجة + الطريقة
الوعد: بعد الفيديو ده هتعرف/هتقدر ...
الإشارة اللي عايزينها: مشاهدة كاملة / إعادة / شير / سيف / كومنت / فولو / ليد
متغير التجربة: ...  (the one thing this video tests: hook type, format, length, CTA …)
```
Signal → what to build in:
- **Full watch / rewatch** (heaviest): short, dense, no dead second, an ending that loops into the first frame, a reveal in the last 3 s.
- **Share**: identity («احنا المصريين…»), relatable pain, a surprising fact people want to send, «ابعته لحد …».
- **Save**: a list, steps, a checklist, a prompt, numbers they'll need later, «احفظه علشان …».
- **Comment**: a specific question, a comparison invitation («انت بتعمل ايه؟»), a fair controversy, «اكتبلي … وأعملهولك».
- **Follow / lead**: series promise («الحلقة الجاية …»), Instagram keyword → DM, full video QR.

## Step 1 — hooks (write 3, pick 1)
Rules: visual > text > verbal (the first frame must work muted; then the on-screen text; then the line). No greeting, no intro, no name
before the hook. The hook is a PROMISE (explicit «تعالى أقولك إزاي…» or implicit: a result, a strange picture, a tension).
Write them as a ladder and keep the best:
- سيء: topic announcement («هكلمكوا عن الـ AI في المونتاج»).
- أحسن: one promise or one tension («انت بتدفع على أدوات AI مش محتاجها»).
- الأحسن: 2-3 stacked concrete promises + a number or personal stake + the viewer's result
  («الفيديو ده اتعمل كله بالـ AI في 5 أيام، بـ 39 credit، ومن غير ما أقف قدام كاميرا — وهوريك إزاي تعمل زيه»).
Do the PAUSE TEST on each: stop after the hook — is not knowing the rest painful?
For each hook also write: the FIRST FRAME (what is seen at 0.0 s) and the ON-SCREEN TEXT (≤ 6 words).
Hook techniques to choose from: pattern interrupt · open loop · specificity · stakes · reframe · fast proof (`techniques.md`).
Aly's existing hook rules still hold: tutorial short = before→after demo first; a short's hook is NEVER the sentence it starts with
after the rewind (take it from later); no two shorts in a series with the same hook.

## Step 2 — body
- List the key points in delivery order; each point = claim → something ON SCREEN (`==…==`, `[شاشة]`, `[B-roll]`) → why it matters to
  the viewer → a handover line to the next point (PACING).
- Decide for each section what it does with the promise: keep it (deliver) · one-up it (over-deliver: «وكمان…») · new promise (extend:
  «بس ده مكانش الدرس الوحيد…»). Plan at least one one-up and, in longer pieces, one new promise near the middle.
- Micro-hook map: mark `[ميكرو هوك]` every 10-15 s in shorts; in long videos a re-hook every 60-90 s and ≤ 3 mini resets.
- Proof at the doubt moments (`[إثبات]`): right after every bold claim — a real screenshot, his bill, his render, a source page.
- REDUNDANCY pass: delete any sentence/visual that says something already said. Compression pass: cut weightless words.
- Vary sentence length (rhythm), and use contrast for every concept that has a wrong way.
- Concepts before names (delayed labeling). Egyptian/local example in every section when possible.

## Step 3 — CTA + exit
- One CTA, after the last value, matched to the type: A → a specific comment question or «ابعته لـ…»; B → save / follow for the series;
  C → full video (thumbnail + QR), Instagram keyword DM, «كلمني لو عايز …».
- Follow-CTA templates (Homus, fill the blanks): «نفسك تنتقل من […] لـ […]؟ اعمل Follow عشان تتعلم الخطوات…» · «بتعاني من […]؟
  اعمل Follow…» · «نفسك حد يختصر عليك سنين التجارب؟ اعمل Follow…». Prefer the specific-comment CTA for A content.
- Clean exit: stop. No summary, no «وده كان كل حاجة». Loop the last line into the first frame when it can rewatch naturally.

## Step 4 — pre-post score (before filming / generating)
Score 1-10 each, show the table: Hook · Value · Clarity · Curiosity · Shareability → total /50.
Under 35 → rework the weakest line before production (the course's planned videos sit at 42-43/50).
Also write the post-post targets you will check: views, retention %, comments (what kind), shares (to whom), saves (why), conversion.

## Templates

### Short / reel (≤ 60-90 s; never ≥ 3:00)
```
[بطاقة النوع والوعد — Step 0]
الهوك (0-3 ث): أول فريم: … | نص على الشاشة: … | الجملة: …
الوعد يتأكد (3-8 ث): …
نقطة 1 (…ث): … ==مرئي== [ميكرو هوك] …
نقطة 2 …                                [إثبات] …
الزيادة (one-up) …
الخروج: CTA سطر واحد → نهاية / لوب
سكور قبل النشر: هوك _ / قيمة _ / وضوح _ / فضول _ / شير _ = _/50
```

### Long YouTube video
YouTube long-form adds packaging (title + thumbnail + first 5 s must match) and CTR/AVD (see aly-content-assistant
`growth-and-retention.md`). Map the reach method onto it:
```
[بطاقة النوع والوعد] (long videos are usually B; their shorts carry the A)
التغليف: 3 عناوين (stake / curiosity / search) + ثمبنيل
الهوك (0-30 ث): أقوى نتيجة/مشهد أولا → الوعد بـ 2-3 وعود مرصوصة → لوب مفتوح للآخر
الخريطة (signposting): الأجزاء اللي جاية في ثانيتين (our TreeMap/breadcrumb)
لكل جزء: claim → مرئي → ليه يهمك → re-hook كل 60-90 ث، one-up في النص، وعد جديد قبل آخر تلت
الإثباتات عند لحظات الشك
الخاتمة: اقفل اللوب → CTA واحد مربوط بفيديو تاني/سلسلة → خروج نظيف
شورتس من الفيديو: (shorts-from-long.md) — اكتبها وانت بتكتب السكريبت مش بعده
```
