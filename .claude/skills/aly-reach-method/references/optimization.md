# The optimization loop (after every batch of ~10 posts)

«Unlocked after 10 posts». Every number is feedback — including the ones that are missing. Ratios beat raw numbers.
Tool: `scripts/reach_batch.py` (CSV → ratios, course points, outliers, markdown report + xlsx). Data comes from Aly (YouTube Studio /
TikTok / Instagram insights exports or numbers he pastes). Read-only on his accounts; never log in or click for him.

## Phase 1 — data per video
views · likes · comments · shares · saves · follows (+ retention %, full-watch / rewatch if the platform shows them) + type A/B/C,
format, hook type, length, notes.

## Phase 2 — outliers
Top 10-20% = positive outliers (study, repeat, but don't build the whole strategy on one) · bottom 10-20% = negative (learn, don't
panic) · the middle 60-80% = CORE performers, where the most reliable patterns live. Judge on ratios, not views alone.

## Phase 3 — pattern analysis
Topics · Hooks · Formats · Delivery style · CTA — one specific observation each.
Ratio reading: high comment rate = interactive / controversial; low = forgettable · high share rate = people send it (relatable /
identity) · low share = fun but not relatable · saves vs likes: 1,000 saves on 5,000 likes = good, on 50,000 = bad (value not kept).

## Phase 4 — comment research
Questions (= next videos) · Objections (= content to handle them) · Confusion (= a simple explainer is needed) · Demand (= series /
offer). One comment can become a hook, a video, a series or an offer improvement.

## Phase 5 — 3 hypotheses (testable sentences)
«[Content] performs better when [condition] because [reason].» Observe → hypothesize → test → repeat. «رأيك في طز» — only the test decides.

## Phase 6 — next 10 videos
| # | topic | hook | test variable | — every video tests one thing (salary-specific vs generic, problem-first hook, exact size/number,
emotional barrier, comparison, contrarian angle, template CTA, comment loop «I answered 5 of your comments»).
Pre-post score /50 for the planned ones; post-post targets (views, retention, which comments, shares to whom, saves why, conversions).

## Weekly review (5 questions)
What worked? What didn't? What patterns do I see? What are people asking for? What will I test next week?
→ write the durable lesson into aly-content-assistant `feedback-log.md` (and this skill if it changes the method).

## Lessons from the 5 worked cases (all point the same way)
- Definitions, terminology, «types of X», anatomy, «history of X» were the negative outliers in every niche — «feels like school», «no
  immediate benefit». Winners: specific life decisions with real numbers, mistakes + fix, visible transformation / before-after,
  emotional moments, identity («why Egyptians struggle with English»), direct correction («stop saying this»).
- Hooks that start with a DECISION or a stuck person («how much…», «what I would do with 100,000 EGP», «why you're not losing weight»,
  «your room isn't small, your layout is wrong») beat hooks that start with a definition.
- Speak to the buyer's reality (Egyptian salaries, small Cairo apartments, beginners' fear), not to peers (photographers talking lenses).
- CTA: a specific comment prompt > «follow for more»; «send this to …» → shares; «save this for …» → saves; «book now» too early for cold.
**For Aly (history / tech / design topics):** never package a history or tech video as a definition or «تاريخ الـ…». Attach it to a
today-decision, an identity («احنا»), a mistake, a surprise or a transformation: not «تاريخ الجرافيكس» but «ليه الفرعوني رسم الناس
بالجنب؟»; not «ايه هو الـ codec» but «ليه الفيديو بتاعك بيبوظ على الواتساب».
